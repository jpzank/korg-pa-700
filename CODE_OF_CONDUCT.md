# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in Arranger Lab a harassment-free experience
for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience or ability.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive and specific.
- Welcome questions and different levels of experience.
- Focus criticism on ideas, code and evidence, not people.
- Respect privacy, copyright, consent and responsible disclosure.
- Accept maintainer decisions made to protect contributors, users or hardware.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, doxxing, publishing
private information, deliberate disruption and bad-faith conduct are not
acceptable in project spaces or when representing the project.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately through a repository maintainer's GitHub
profile. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or
permanently restrict participation when behavior is inappropriate, threatening
or harmful. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical.
